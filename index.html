Who we are

A club built on loyalty, not just runs

We started in 2021 with borrowed gear and a shared idea: build a side where nobody gets left on the bench for being new, and nobody gets carried for being old friends.

Today Al Fatah Cricket Club fields a full competitive squad, runs its own training program, and backs players from junior age all the way to senior grade. We recruit for character first and coach the cricket second, which is exactly why the dressing room stays as strong as the scorecard.

Our home is Melbourne. Our colours are green and gold. Our record speaks for itself, and our next season starts now.

The season ahead

Three leagues for the green and gold

Full match centre
01

Darebin Chargers League

DCL T10, Group C with Darebin Lions, Tarneit Central, Melbourne Warriors and Wollert Rhinos. Ten overs a side and already underway.

02

NWS League 2026/27

Twelve clubs, two pools. Al Fatah is in Pool B, five pool games then quarter finals, semis and the grand final. Starts 30 August 2026.

03

Truganina Premier League

The 7th TPL, T20 Friendship Cup. Fourteen teams. We go in defending the trophy we won in 2025. Starts 4 October 2026.

What we stand for

Four things we never compromise

One Team

Selection is on merit and effort. Nobody gets a spot on reputation and nobody loses one on a single bad day.

Focused

Every session has a purpose. We plan matches properly, review them honestly, and fix what needs fixing.

Play With Heart

We win with honour and we lose with our heads up. The handshake at the end matters as much as the result.

Together We Achieve

Trophies are shared. So are the ground fees, the transport, the kit runs and the early Sunday starts.
