This season

Three competitions, one squad

Round by round dates are released by each league as the season runs. Match day squads and venues go out through the club WhatsApp group.

01

Darebin Chargers League

The DCL T10 competition. Al Fatah is drawn in Group C alongside Darebin Lions, Tarneit Central, Melbourne Warriors and Wollert Rhinos. Ten overs a side, so a single over can decide it.

T10Group CUnderway
02

NWS League 2026/27

Twelve clubs across two pools. Al Fatah sits in Pool B with FCC Red, Knight Riders, Reservoir Rangers, Mel United and Northside Falcons. Five pool games, then quarter finals, semi finals and the grand final.

T20Pool BFrom 30 Aug 2026
03

7th Truganina Premier League

The T20 Friendship Cup, run by Truganina Cricket Club. Fourteen teams, seven round games, then quarters, semis and the final. Al Fatah goes in as the 2025 Friendship Cup winner.

T2014 teamsFrom 4 Oct 2026
