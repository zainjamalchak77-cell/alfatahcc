Play for us

Register your interest

Open to all levels. Fill this in and the captain or vice captain will come back to you about training and trials.

We use your details only to contact you about playing for the club.

What happens next

You get a reply from the club, usually within a few days. If the timing suits we will invite you to a training session so you can meet the group and have a bat and a bowl.

There is no trial fee and no obligation. Plenty of players come to one session, decide it is not for them, and that is completely fine.

Common questions

Do I need my own gear?
No. Bring what you have and the club will cover the rest for a first session.

What does it cost to play?
There are season fees that cover registration, grounds and match costs. The club will give you the exact figure before you commit to anything.

I have not played in years. Is that a problem?
No. Several of our squad came back to cricket after long breaks. Turn up and see how you go.

Can I just come and watch first?
Yes. Message the club and we will tell you where the next match is.
