Squad & leadership

Twenty four players across two competition squads. Leadership sets the standard, the squad meets it, and the door stays open for players who want to earn a place.

Who runs Al Fatah

President & Captain

Faisal Hayat

Founded the club in 2021 and has captained it ever since. Leads both the NWS T20 and DCL T10 squads, and runs selection.

Vice Captain & Senior Analyst

Tahir Saeed

Deputises for the captain and owns match preparation. Opposition analysis, field settings, bowling plans and post match review all run through him.
